Asked & answered

A few honest FAQs.

Is it really free? +
Yes, completely. There's a "tip jar" link in every issue if you want to chip in for hosting, but it's never expected.
How often will I hear from you? +
Once a week, on Sunday mornings (in your timezone). Occasionally I'll skip a week if I have nothing worth saying.
Will you share my email? +
Never. Your email lives in a Postgres table on a server I personally run. I don't use a third-party email provider that scrapes your data.
Can I unsubscribe easily? +
One click at the bottom of every email. No confirmation page, no "are you sure?" survey, no dark patterns.
Is there an RSS feed? +
Of course. Right here. The web is better when feeds are first-class.
